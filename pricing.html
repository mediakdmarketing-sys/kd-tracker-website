Questions

Package questions

Do I need a card for the free trial?

No. Start the 14-day trial with just your email. You add payment details only if you decide to keep a paid package.

What counts as a person?

Anyone with an active account who tracks time. Admins and HR who only manage the team are free on every plan.

What if we go over our people limit?

We let you know and help you move to the next package. Nobody is locked out in the middle of a working day.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes. Switch at renewal and the yearly discount applies from the next billing period.

Do you have discounts for non-profits or education?

Yes, on request. Tell us about your organisation and we will find a fair price.

How does agency pricing work?

Agencies get a plan, trial and limits per client company, plus white-label options. Pricing depends on how many companies and people you manage, so please get in touch.

Not sure which package fits?

Tell us about your team and we will recommend one.
