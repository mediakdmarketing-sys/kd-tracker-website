FAQ Questions

Good questions, straight answers

Is it legal to monitor employees?

Monitoring is lawful in many places when it is disclosed and proportionate. WorkBuddy shows every employee exactly what is recorded and asks for consent before the first shift. Check local rules and your employment contracts, and ask us if you want to talk it through.

Does it record what people type?

No. It counts keystrokes and mouse activity and notes the active application. The content of what is typed is never captured or stored.

What if someone does not want audio?

Audio is optional and off unless the employee agrees. They can turn it off again at any time, and nothing is recorded while it is off.

What happens when the power or internet goes out?

The desktop app keeps working offline and uploads when the connection returns. If a person punches in again the same day, their balance carries on rather than starting over.

Can we choose what counts as a half day?

Yes. HR sets the hours for a half day and a full day, plus break allowances, idle rules and how long captures are kept.

How is our data separated from other companies?

Each company's people, files and settings are isolated. Admins can only see their own company, and any attempt to reach another company's records is refused.

Can we leave and take our data?

Yes. A full export of your records is available when a contract ends, and your data can be removed permanently on request.
